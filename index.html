How to play

It takes 3 to score: connect 3+ same-color tiles to score and clear them. Stars from your rack are wild and permanent. Drag your 3-tile pieces onto the board (tap again or ↻ to rotate). Place 0–3 pieces anywhere they fit on empty squares, then PLAY. ⚡ Power-ups (2 per game, 1 per move): Double Points, Bomb (3×3) or Cross Blast (row + column). Blasts clear everything in the area, even stars, but score nothing. Coins: start with 100, win = +10. Each game has 1 free swap and 2 free power-ups; extra swaps or power-ups cost 50 coins. 10 moves each — higher score wins!
