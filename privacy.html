Star Tiles – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Star Tiles (“the App”) is a casual puzzle game published by Dhaval Patel. This policy explains what information the App handles.

What we collect

We do not sell or share your data for advertising, and there is no analytics or tracking SDK.

Guest play (default): no account is created. Progress stays on your device only.

Optional Sign in with Apple: if you choose to sign in, Apple may share a unique account id and (only if you allow it) your name and a private relay email with us. We store a profile on our database (Supabase): display name, coins, game stats, daily missions and login streak, linked only to that Apple account id. You can sign out or delete the account from the Account screen; deletion removes the cloud profile and the sign-in.

Data stored on your device

Game progress, coins, settings (including mute and difficulty), daily missions, and your display name for local or online play are saved on your device (for example in local storage). This data never leaves your device except as described below for online play.

Online play

If you start or join an online game, the App sends short, anonymous game messages (room code, moves, chat text you type, and a random session id) through public internet relays (currently MQTT over WebSocket and ntfy.sh) so the other phone can sync the same game. Messages are not linked to your identity. Do not put personal information in chat. Relays may briefly retain messages as part of how they work; we do not operate those relays and do not log them ourselves.

Internet access

Internet access is used only for optional online multiplayer and, on the web version, to load the game. Single-player and pass-and-play modes work offline in the App.

Children

The App is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on the public project at github.com/Dhpa69/star-tiles-game, or contact the developer through App Store Connect once the App is listed.
