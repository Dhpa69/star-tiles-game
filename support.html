How to play

Drop 3-tile pieces on the board and connect 3+ tiles of one color to score. Stars are wild. x2/x3 squares multiply the group. Each player gets 10 moves.

Purchases

Coin packs, Remove Ads and Pro are bought through Apple. Changed phones? Open the Coin shop and tap Restore purchases (restores Remove Ads / Pro). For refunds use reportaproblem.apple.com.

Online play & chat

Online games are private: only someone you give a room code to can join. In chat use 🚩 to report and ⛔ to block a player. You can turn chat off on the home screen (💬 Chat: OFF).

Account

Signing in with Apple is optional. To delete your account: Home → 👤 Account → Delete account. This removes your cloud profile and sign-in immediately.

Notifications

Turn notifications tell you when it's your move in an online game. Turn them off any time in iPhone Settings → Notifications → Star Tiles.
