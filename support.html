How to play

Drop 3-tile pieces on the board and connect 3+ tiles of one color to score. Stars are wild. x2/x3 squares multiply the group. Each player gets 10 moves. Power-ups (Double, Triple, Bomb, Cross Blast…) can each be used once per game. Team 2v2 is played on a 10x10 board with 10 moves per player.

Purchases

Coin packs, Remove Ads and Pro are bought through Apple. Changed phones? Open the Coin shop and tap Restore purchases (restores Remove Ads / Pro). For refunds use reportaproblem.apple.com.

Online play & chat

Online games are private: only someone you give a room code to can join. In chat use 🚩 to report and ⛔ to block a player. You can turn chat off on the home screen (💬 Chat: OFF).

Account

No account is needed; signing in with Apple is optional and only syncs progress. To delete your account: Home → 👤 Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your cloud profile, sign-in and linked leaderboard entries. You can also email us to request deletion.

Notifications

Turn notifications tell you when it's your move in an online game. Turn them off any time in iPhone Settings → Notifications → Star Tiles.
